Some seasons, the best thing to do with bitcoin is nothing.

Fallow is a weekly rotation strategy across BTC, ETH and three Bitget rTokens: the S&P 500, gold and long Treasuries. It holds whichever are trending and keeps the rest in USDT. It trades spot only and uses no leverage.

The name is a farming word. A fallow field is one you leave unplanted until it is worth sowing again.

Signal
Time-series momentum. For each asset, the share of four lookbacks (21, 63, 126 and 252 days) with a positive return.
Sizing
Each asset gets an equal slice of a 15% yearly volatility target, scaled by its signal and its 60-day volatility. Exposure is capped at 100%, and every position is long.
Rebalance
Weekly. The signal is read at midday on Monday in New York and traded one daily bar later.

How the weights are set

  1. 01

    Score the trend

    The rule asks whether the price is higher than it was one, three, six and twelve months ago. Each yes adds a quarter to the asset's score. Four out of four means a full position, and none means the asset is not held.

  2. 02

    Size by volatility

    Every asset held should carry about the same risk. Bitcoin swings hard, so it rarely gets more than a tenth of the account, even in a good run. The account as a whole targets volatility of about 15% a year.

  3. 03

    Keep the rest in USDT

    Whatever is not allocated stays in USDT. Fallow never borrows and never shorts. The weights are set once a week from Monday's midday price in New York and traded a day later.

Where it loses

These are the things that should make you hesitate.

    How much the settings matter

    We ran versions of the rule with different lookbacks, volatility windows, risk budgets and rebalance days. Each tick is one version's out-of-sample Sharpe. The tall mark is the version on this page.

    How to reproduce it

    Everything here comes from Bitget's public candle API and about 500 lines of Python with no dependencies.

    python3 -m fallow fetch     # daily candles for nine symbols
python3 -m fallow report    # every number on this page
python3 -m fallow plan      # this week's weights
python3 -m unittest discover tests

    Things to know about the data

    • Bitcoin and ether candles are Bitget spot, from July 2018.
    • rToken spot pairs went live on . Bitget serves earlier candles for those symbols too. They are the price of the underlying fund, without dividends.
    • Daily bars open at 16:00 UTC. A day when New York is shut carries the last price forward.
    • USDT earns nothing in the backtest.
    • Costs are 15 bp for each unit traded.
